About

I'm Maryna, a product designer.

I design internal tools, workflow software and AI features, from research to release. Besides design, I plan the work and bring design processes into how the team works.

I started in 2015 as a web designer with a front-end background. What I still use from that time is my knowledge of HTML and CSS.

Alongside my job, I design and build my own products with AI tools, such as Pagecraft, to experiment with and test AI in my design process.

Process

How I work

It depends on the project, but I usually follow the same steps.

01

Gather information

I talk to stakeholders, developers, support and users, and look at competitors and what's already on the market.

02

Structure it

I sort what I've collected. Sometimes there's a lot of data, sometimes very little and I work from assumptions.

03

Propose options

A couple of proposals, reviewed together with the team, including a technical check, so we know it can be built.

04

Test and improve

Rounds of testing and changes.

05

Final design and release

I work with the developers until release.

Leading the design process. I'm responsible for the design of the product I work on. Besides delivering designs, I plan the design process and make it part of how the team works.

AI in my process. I now use AI at every stage of design. I'm still working out how to make it better and more efficient, but it's already part of how I work.
