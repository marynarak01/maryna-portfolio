ABOUT THIS SCREEN

A separate "Images" section, kept out of the copy builder so it stays clean. The URL picker is the main path; upload is the always-visible fallback so users are never blocked.

HOW THE PICKER WORKS

We don't guess which product to carry over — there are too many links. Focusing the empty field shows recent links; typing a word searches the link database. Pasting a full URL just closes the dropdown and fills the field — no extra "use this link" step.

AI BEHAVIOUR

On a successful pull, AI reads each image in the background so it's ready to match to the product's photo types on the next step.
