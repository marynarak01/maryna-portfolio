WHY NOT JUST MAKE IT RESPONSIVE

Desktop puts the URL field, thumbnails and upload zone on one view. On a phone they stack into a scroll — that's fine here, so Step 1 survives the squeeze. Step 2 does not.

What changes is control sizing and placement, not structure. Every target is ≥44pt, and Continue moves to a fixed bottom bar.

THUMB ZONE

Continue is pinned to the bottom, not at the end of the scroll. On a 6"+ phone the top 40% is a stretch one-handed; the primary action belongs in the thumb arc.

Back sits top-left — infrequent, and safe from accidental taps. Edge-swipe back still works.

TOUCH TARGETS

URL field is 52pt tall, recents rows 56pt, Continue 52pt — all above the 44pt minimum.

The clear (×) button is visually 22pt but has a 44pt hit area. Visual size and tap size are separate.

Input font is 16px — below that, iOS auto-zooms on focus and the layout jumps.

MOBILE-ONLY CHANGES

The desktop rail (Add images → Build gallery → Review) becomes a 3-segment progress bar. A vertical rail would eat a third of the width.

Recents show 3, not 8. Mobile cognitive load: fewer options per screen, and the field is right there to search.

Thumbnails are a 3-up grid, tap-to-remove. No hover, so the × is always visible.

"Upload" opens the native picker — camera roll, files, or take a photo. On mobile the camera is a real source; the desktop drop zone is not.
